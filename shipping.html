Shipping & Delivery

This page is part of a prototype and does not represent real shipping services. It is written to show how a luxury jewellery brand like Lunari might describe its shipping and delivery options on a live store.

In a real store, Lunari could process orders within 1–2 business days. During busy periods or for made‑to‑order pieces, processing might take longer before the jewellery ships from the studio or warehouse.

A typical brand might offer standard tracked shipping of 3–5 business days within the same region, and 7–14 business days for international destinations, depending on the carrier and location.

For this prototype, all prices are for display only. A real Lunari store could offer free standard shipping above a certain order value and a paid express option, with costs varying by destination and service level.

High‑value jewellery is usually shipped in discreet, protective packaging and may include insured, signature‑on‑delivery services. This prototype mentions these options for realism but does not ship physical items.

Orders and tracking are simulated only. Any cart contents or sample details you enter are stored locally in your browser using localStorage to support the demo experience and can be cleared at any time.
