Jewellery Care Guide

Simple steps to help keep your Lunari pieces shining for everyday wear.

Everyday Care

Put jewellery on last, after perfume, lotions, hair products, and makeup have fully dried to minimise contact with chemicals that can dull metal and stones.

Remove pieces before showering, swimming, exercising, cleaning, or sleeping to avoid moisture, sweat, impact, and abrasion.

Cleaning Your Jewellery

  • Use lukewarm water, a drop of mild soap, and a soft brush for most metal and stone pieces, then pat dry with a soft lint‑free cloth.
  • Avoid harsh chemicals, alcohol wipes, toothpaste, or abrasive cloths, which can scratch metal or strip gold plating.
  • For pieces that look heavily tarnished or very delicate, choose a professional clean and inspection instead of aggressive at‑home methods.

Sterling Silver & Gold‑Plated Pieces

  • Sterling silver may naturally tarnish; gentle, regular wiping with a soft polishing cloth helps keep it bright and reduces buildup.
  • Gold‑plated pieces benefit from extra care: limit water and friction, and clean with a soft dry cloth rather than strong cleaners to help the finish last longer.

Storage & Travel

  • Store jewellery in a dry, closed box or pouch, away from bathrooms and direct sunlight, to reduce moisture and oxidation.
  • Keep pieces separate so chains do not tangle and metals or stones do not rub and scratch each other during storage or travel.

When to Contact Support

If you notice loose stones, damaged clasps, or heavy wear on plating, pause wearing the piece and contact Lunari support for repair or replacement options before further damage occurs.
