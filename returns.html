Returns & Exchanges

This page is part of a prototype and does not apply to real purchases. It is written to show how a luxury jewellery brand like Lunari might explain its return and exchange rules in production.

In a real store, Lunari could offer a 14‑day return period from the delivery date for most jewellery, as long as the item is unworn, undamaged, and returned with original box, paperwork, and tags attached.

Custom‑engraved pieces, resized rings, and one‑of‑a‑kind designs are typically non‑returnable. In this prototype they are mentioned to reflect common jewellery policies, but no real transactions occur here.

A typical process would be: contact support with your order number, receive a return authorisation, send the piece back using an insured, trackable service, and wait for inspection before the exchange or store credit is confirmed.

For real high‑value jewellery, brands often require insured shipping and allow several business days for quality control checks. This prototype mirrors that language but does not accept or ship physical items.

Checkout, login and returns are simulated only. Any usernames or emails you enter are stored locally in your browser to support the demo experience and can be removed by clearing localStorage or your browsing data.
